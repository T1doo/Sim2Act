Before a practice station may be labelled ready, both its guard and its checklist need a recorded inspection.
A damaged guard makes the station unavailable, even if the checklist passed.
For a reserved station, replace the ready label with reserved; the inspection requirement still applies before use.
A missing inspection record calls for a review-needed label, never an assumed pass.
Who may clear review-needed, how long a reservation lasts, and the label for a reserved station with a damaged guard are left open.
